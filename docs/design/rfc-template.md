# RFC: Short proposal title

- Status: Proposed
- Authors: Name or team
- Reviewers: Affected owners
- Created: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision deadline: YYYY-MM-DD
- Related: Issue/ADR/PR links

## Summary

State the problem, proposed outcome, and recommendation in a few sentences.

## Context and evidence

Describe current behavior, constraints, users, measurements, incidents, and assumptions. Explain why now.

## Goals and non-goals

### Goals

- Define measurable outcomes.

### Non-goals

- Bound the proposal explicitly.

## Proposed design

Describe behavior, components, contracts, data flow, trust boundaries, failure handling, observability, deployment, and ownership. Add a diagram only when it clarifies relationships.

## Alternatives

Compare credible alternatives, including doing nothing. Explain tradeoffs and reversibility rather than listing features.

## Security, privacy, and abuse

Identify assets, actors, untrusted inputs, permissions, sensitive data, misuse cases, and controls. Link the threat-model change.

## Data and compatibility

Describe schema/API changes, migration, retention, deletion, idempotency, backward/forward compatibility, and recovery.

## Rollout and rollback

Define flags, cohorts, monitoring, success/stop signals, deployment order, and rollback or roll-forward steps.

## Test and validation plan

Map goals and failure modes to unit, integration, end-to-end, platform, load, security, and live checks. State what cannot be validated before rollout.

## Open questions

| Question | Owner | Due | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Define | Define | Define | Open |

## Decision

Complete after review: outcome, date, decision makers, rejected alternatives, and follow-up ADRs/actions.
