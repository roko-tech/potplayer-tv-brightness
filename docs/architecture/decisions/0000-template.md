# ADR-0000: Short decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: Name or team
- Related: Issue/RFC/PR links

## Context

What forces, constraints, evidence, and risks make a decision necessary? Separate known facts from assumptions.

## Decision drivers

- The outcomes and constraints that matter most.

## Considered options

### Option A

Describe benefits, costs, risks, and reversibility.

### Option B

Describe benefits, costs, risks, and reversibility.

## Decision

State the chosen option and scope precisely.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expected benefit.

### Negative and tradeoffs

- Accepted cost or limitation.

### Follow-up

- Migration, measurement, review date, or trigger to reconsider.

## Validation

What evidence will show that the decision works? Include benchmarks, experiments, tests, or operational measures when applicable.
