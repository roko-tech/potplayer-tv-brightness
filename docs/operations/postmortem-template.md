## Executive summary

State user impact, duration, affected scope, and current status without blame or unsupported certainty.

## Impact

Quantify failed/degraded user journeys, data/security impact, support burden, and SLO/error-budget effect. Label estimates.

## Detection and response

How was the incident detected? What signals were missing or misleading? What mitigated it and how was recovery verified?

## Timeline

Use timestamped observed events. Separate facts from later inference.

## Contributing conditions and root cause

Explain the technical and organizational conditions that allowed the impact. Avoid stopping at the final human action or component failure.

## What worked and what did not

Review prevention, detection, diagnosis, mitigation, communication, and recovery controls.
